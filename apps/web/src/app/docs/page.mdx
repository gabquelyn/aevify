# Aevify documentation

Aevify is a stablecoin checkout for online stores and AI agents. Customers pay in **tUSD8** on
**QDay Aevum**, payments settle straight to your wallet, and your server finds out through signed
webhooks. You never handle wallets, gas or contract calls yourself.

> Aevify runs on Aevum **Testnet** for the Aevum Builder Challenge. tUSD8 and tQDAY are test
> tokens with no value.

## How it works

1. **Your server** creates a payment with your secret key (REST API or `@aevify/node`).
2. **Your customer** pays on the hosted checkout: a link, a redirect, or a modal from `@aevify/react`.
3. **The Aevify contract** on Aevum takes the payment and sends 97% straight to your payout wallet. Aevify never holds funds.
4. **Your server** gets a signed `payment.paid` webhook, verifies it, and fulfils the order.

AI agents pay the same checkout in code, optionally locked so only a specific agent wallet can pay.

## Keys at a glance

| Key | Where it lives | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Secret key `sk_test_…` | Your server only | Creating and reading payments. Shown once; Aevify stores only a hash. |
| Publishable key `pk_test_…` | Your frontend | Lets `@aevify/react` embed your checkout on your allowed domains |
| Webhook secret `whsec_…` | Your server | Proves a webhook came from Aevify |

Create them in the [dashboard](/dashboard) under **Developers**.

## Fees

3% per payment, taken by the contract at settlement. The contract can never charge more than 5%,
not even if Aevify's own admin key changes it.
